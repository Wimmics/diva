Case Study: eSTIMe

The DIVA ontology was evaluated using interaction logs from a user study involving six mobility experts exploring urban mobility data with the eSTIMe multi-view visualization system. During approximately one hour of open-ended exploration, participants investigated travel purposes and transportation modes, the spatial and temporal distribution of populations, and individual activity patterns. Their exploration paths ranged from 31 to 207 steps. The logs, originally recorded as structured JSON arrays containing interaction events, timestamps, visualization states, and related metadata, were transformed into an RDF knowledge graph comprising 22,048 triples using the rdflib library. To demonstrate the graph’s analytical capabilities, three competency questions were implemented as SPARQL queries, with results visualized in Muvin. These queries explored the chronological sequence of individual actions, similarities between users’ exploration strategies, and patterns in the use of visualization techniques. The case study demonstrates how DIVA supports querying, comparing, and interpreting user exploration behaviors.
