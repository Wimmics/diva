About the thesaurus

The DIVA thesauri provide controlled vocabularies for describing and classifying concepts related to user interactions and visualization systems, including event and action types, view manipulations, visual marks, channels, and user-interface elements. They support consistent semantic annotation of interaction data and facilitate its integration and analysis across visualization systems.
